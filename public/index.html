    How the generations model works: each generation is the previous one × (fertility rate ÷ 2.1), where 2.1 births per woman keeps a population stable. A generation is about 30 years. It deliberately ignores migration, changing life expectancy and age-structure momentum, so it shows the long-run pull of a fertility rate, not a forecast. The UN projection on the trend chart is the forecast.
